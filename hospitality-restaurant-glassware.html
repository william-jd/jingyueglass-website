Operational Baseline · Casual & High-Traffic

Commercial Soda-Lime Glass

The industry benchmark for casual dining, high-turnover bars, gastropubs, and banquet rental services where mechanical strength, drop resilience, and replacement economy are paramount.

  • Primary Strength: Exceptional mechanical impact resistance & heavy-weight anti-tipping base
  • Thermal Durability: Thermal shock resistance up to ΔT 60°C for commercial washers
  • Cost Efficiency: Low replacement cost with flexible master carton replenishment
  • Recommended For: Water tumblers, beer glasses, highball cocktails, all-day dining
Standard SKUs: XZ5170-3 (350ml), XZ1280 (345ml), XZ075 (440ml)
Elevated Dining · Fine Dining & Lounges

Refined Lead-Free Crystal Glass

Selected for fine dining, Michelin-starred venues, luxury hotel suites, and dedicated wine bars requiring pristine optical transmission, acoustic resonance, and beadless cold-cut rims.

  • Sensory Quality: Ultra-thin 0.8mm laser cold-cut rim for enhanced aroma and palate entry
  • Optical Brilliance: High refractive index without lead oxide content (FDA/LFGB compliant)
  • Acoustic Resonance: Clean harmonic ring during table toasts
  • Recommended For: Bordeaux/Burgundy stemware, champagne flutes, whiskey tasting
Standard SKUs: XZ6301 (420ml), XZ6304 (200ml)

Procurement FAQ

Hospitality Buyer Questions

How should hospitality buyers choose between soda-lime glass and lead-free crystal?
For high-traffic casual dining, bistro chains, and banquet rental where dishwashing turnover and impact resistance are primary, commercial soda-lime glass provides the most economical and durable baseline. For fine dining, executive hotel suites, and premium cocktail bars, lead-free crystal offers higher optical brilliance, acoustic resonance, and ultra-fine cold-cut rims.
What is the standard carton packing and MOQ for hospitality reorders?
Standard catalog items are packed in master export cartons (e.g. 36 or 48 pcs/carton). Reorder MOQ starts from standard master cartons, and we support mixed-SKU container consolidation (FCL/LCL) combining tumblers, wine glasses, whiskey rocks, and beer glasses in a single shipment.
Can you apply custom venue logos and certified pour lines?
Yes. We offer permanent 580°C kiln-fired ceramic decals, bottom laser engraving, and certified portion-control fill lines (e.g. 125ml, 175ml, 250ml, 1.5oz, 2oz) resistant to commercial alkaline dishwashing chemicals.
How do you guarantee safe transit and replace broken glassware?
Shipments are packed in ISTA-1A drop-tested 5-ply cartons with reinforced partitions and shrink-wrapped palletization. We provide a 100% free replacement or credit warranty for any transit damage reported within 7 days of port arrival.
