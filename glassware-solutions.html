Quality & Compliance

Documentation for Product-Specific Discussions

Quality and food-contact documentation should match the selected SKU, material, decoration and market. We can discuss the applicable documents during quotation.

FDA food-contact reportsSample reports covering leachable lead and cadmium under stated US FDA test scopes.
LFGB test reportsSample reports covering stated LFGB and Regulation (EC) No. 1935/2004 scopes.
Finished product inspectionDiscuss inspection points such as clarity, wall thickness, rim, packing and decoration durability.
Important: Test reports apply to the tested samples and stated scopes. They are not blanket certification for every product or future production batch. Ask us to confirm the applicable documentation for your SKU and market.

FAQ

Glassware Sourcing Solutions FAQ

Can I combine multiple glassware categories in one project?

Yes, compatible available standard SKUs can be discussed for a mixed sourcing project. Final quantities, packing, availability and shipping arrangement are confirmed with the selected items before quotation.

Can you add our logo and create retail packaging?

Yes. Decoration and packaging options are evaluated according to the glass SKU, artwork, durability requirement, order quantity and target market. The suitable process and MOQ are confirmed before quotation.

What should I send for a complete quotation?

Send the product or reference image, estimated quantity by style, logo artwork, packaging requirements, destination country or port and target delivery date. This helps us recommend the correct sourcing route.

Do you support supermarket and hospitality buyers?

Yes. The product range and sourcing routes are designed for distributors, supermarket buyers, hospitality suppliers and brands. Please specify the sales channel so we can align the product and packaging discussion.
