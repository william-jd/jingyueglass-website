FAQ
Common Questions
Are your glass mugs safe for hot drinks?
Yes. All our mugs are made from borosilicate glass which is heat-resistant up to 400°C and safe for hot coffee, tea, and other hot beverages. They are also microwave and dishwasher safe.
What is the MOQ for custom logo mugs?
Our MOQ is 36 pcs for most mug styles including custom logo printing. This makes it accessible for small cafés, boutique hotels, and e-commerce sellers.
What logo printing methods do you offer?
We offer screen printing, sandblasting, decal transfer, and laser engraving. The best method depends on your logo design and color requirements. We'll advise you after seeing your artwork.
Can I get the mugs in individual retail packaging?
Yes. We offer individual gift boxes, kraft paper packaging, and foam inserts suitable for retail and e-commerce. Custom box printing is also available.
What is the small silicone point at the bottom of double-wall mugs?
That is a hydrophobic silicone pressure-equalization vent. During hand-blowing, this microscopic vent allows air pressure between the double glass walls to equalize as temperatures fluctuate, preventing cracking in microwave and dishwasher cycles. It is a standard engineering safety feature.
Do you ship to South America and Southeast Asia?
Yes — we regularly ship to Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Thailand, Indonesia, Vietnam, and across Europe. We provide all export documents for customs clearance.

Frequently Asked Procurement Questions

Key commercial terms, quality standards, and OEM customization guidelines for international buyers.

As canecas de café de vidro de parede dupla são feitas de vidro borossilicato de alta qualidade?

Sim, todas as canecas de parede dupla da Jingyue Glass são sopradas à mão em vidro borossilicato 3.3 de grau laboratorial, com tolerância a choque térmico de -20°C a +150°C, seguras para micro-ondas e sem condensação externa.

Como é vedado o orifício de ventilação na base dos copos de parede dupla?

Utilizamos tampões de silicone hidrofóbico de grau médico na base para equilibrar a pressão interna com bebidas quentes ou frias, impedindo a entrada de água da lava-louças na câmara de ar.

É possível incluir flores desidratadas reais, glitter ou miniaturas personalizadas na parede dupla?

Sim, somos especializados em canecas com flores botânicas desidratadas, copos com glitter flutuante e miniaturas 3D de vidro seladas hermeticamente entre as paredes duplas.

Qual é o MOQ e o prazo de envio de amostras para canecas personalizadas?

Para modelos em estoque o MOQ começa em 500 unidades; para impressão de decalque ou arranjos de flores personalizadas o MOQ é de 1.000 unidades com amostras físicas rápidas em 3 a 5 dias.
