Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for Model XZ-0075, and can we order with custom gift packaging?
Standard stock orders start at 24 pcs (3 master cartons). For custom laser logo branding or bespoke retail packaging (such as rigid gift boxes with custom-cut EPE foam), MOQ is 100 pcs.
How does the dual-opening U-shape design improve wine aeration?
Wine poured through the wider opening cascades along the curved crystal body, maximizing oxygen contact. The narrower spout directs a smooth, drip-free laminar stream into wine glasses, making it ideal for young tannic reds and aged vintages.
How do you package this delicate sculptural shape to prevent ocean shipping damage?
Every XZ-0075 decanter is encased in a custom-molded, odorless EPE pearl cotton cradle that fully contours to the twin curves. Cartons are reinforced with 5-ply corrugated walls, corner protectors, and palletized with stretch film (ISTA-1A certified).
Can we apply custom winery logos, crests, or hand-painted gold rims?
Yes. We offer precision frosted laser engraving, 12% genuine liquid gold luster along the rim or base, and custom die-cast metal adhesive crests. Digital proofs are provided within 24 hours.
What are the recommended cleaning and maintenance procedures?
Rinse with warm water and use stainless steel cleaning beads for sediment removal. Air dry on a dedicated decanter stand or wipe with a lint-free microfiber cloth. Hand washing is recommended to preserve crystal luster.
Is Model XZ-0075 certified for food contact safety in US and EU markets?
Yes. The acoustic crystal glass contains 0% lead and 0% cadmium, fully compliant with FDA (21 CFR 175.300), California Prop 65, German LFGB (EC 1935/2004), and EU Directive 84/500/EEC.
