FAQ
Common Questions
Are your glass mugs safe for hot drinks?
Yes. All our mugs are made from borosilicate glass which is heat-resistant up to 400°C and safe for hot coffee, tea, and other hot beverages. They are also microwave and dishwasher safe.
What is the MOQ for custom logo mugs?
Our MOQ is 36 pcs for most mug styles including custom logo printing. This makes it accessible for small cafés, boutique hotels, and e-commerce sellers.
What logo printing methods do you offer?
We offer screen printing, sandblasting, decal transfer, and laser engraving. The best method depends on your logo design and color requirements. We'll advise you after seeing your artwork.
Can I get the mugs in individual retail packaging?
Yes. We offer individual gift boxes, kraft paper packaging, and foam inserts suitable for retail and e-commerce. Custom box printing is also available.
Do you ship to South America and Southeast Asia?
Yes — we regularly ship to Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Thailand, Indonesia, Vietnam, and across Europe. We provide all export documents for customs clearance.
