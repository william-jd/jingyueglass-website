Frequently Asked Questions · Decanter Sourcing

How are crystal decanters packed for safe international transit?

Each decanter is secured inside high-density odorless EPE pearl cotton inserts and 5-ply export master cartons, passing ISTA-1A drop tests with <0.5% breakage rates.

Can we customize logo engraving or presentation gift boxes?

Yes. We support precision CO2 laser etching from 48 pcs, 580°C ceramic decal firing, genuine gold rim detailing, and custom velvet-lined wooden presentation boxes.
