When a mixed-order route makes sense

It is useful when your buyer needs an assortment, a market test or coordinated categories—not an artificially large commitment to one product.

Retail assortment test

Test multiple glass categories or styles before deciding which SKUs deserve a larger reorder.

Distributor replenishment

Plan a replenishment mix that reflects the products a distributor actually needs for different customer segments.

Gift and hospitality program

Coordinate drinkware, decanters or sets with packaging and handling requirements under one brief.

Keep custom work separate from standard-SKU availability

Mixed ordering is easiest when buyers first identify available standard SKUs. Logo decoration, custom packaging or new shapes may introduce separate production conditions, so they should be confirmed as part of the quote rather than assumed to share the same MOQ.

Multi-SKU compatibility: what must be checked first

A mixed container or mixed shipment should not be treated as a simple list of products. The selected SKUs must be checked for carton size, packing method, decoration route, MOQ, accessory count and destination requirements.

Mixed glassware order FAQ

Can I mix different glassware categories in one order?

Compatible available SKUs can be discussed as a mixed order. The final combination is confirmed from item availability, quantity by SKU, carton packing, decoration requirements and destination.

What is the minimum quantity for a mixed order?

For available standard items, low-volume testing may start from 30 pieces per SKU. The final quantity depends on each selected SKU, stock status, packing and any custom work.

What is needed to quote a mixed container or mixed shipment?

Send the selected categories or reference images, estimated quantity per SKU, destination, packaging requirement, branding request and desired delivery timing. This is used to confirm compatibility and the right shipping discussion.

Can all glassware SKUs be mixed in one container?

Not always. Compatibility depends on item availability, carton size, packing method, accessory count, logo or packaging work and destination requirements. Jingyue Glass should review the SKU list before confirming the route.

What risks should I check before confirming a mixed-SKU glassware order?

Check carton quantity, packaging type, SKU labels, barcode files, MOQ by SKU, breakage protection and pre-shipment inspection points. These details reduce receiving and quality-control issues.
