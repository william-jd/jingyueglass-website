How durable is Model XZ5825 under commercial bar and dishwasher use?
Model XZ5825 is engineered with reinforced glass walls and a chip-resistant rim to withstand high-turnover bar handling. It fully endures 1,000+ commercial dishwashing cycles without hazing, etching, or graphic degradation.
Can we print multi-color brewery logos, pour lines, or frosted insignias?

What is the sample dispatch and mass production timeline?
Plain inventory samples ship within 1–2 business days. Custom logo golden samples dispatch in 5–7 days. Bulk production takes 15–20 days depending on decal complexity and carton customization.
Is Model XZ5825 fully certified for US, EU, and global food contact regulations?
Yes. All glass batches are 100% lead-free and cadmium-free, meeting US FDA (21 CFR 175.300), California Prop 65, European LFGB (EC 1935/2004), and EU 84/500/EEC standards. Third-party testing reports are available upon request.
