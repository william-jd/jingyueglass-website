5-Stage Pre-Production Approval SOP

1

Stage 1: Digital 2D/3D Artwork & DFM Review (24 Hours)

Our engineering team verifies your vector logo file, laser engraving coordinates, capacity tolerances, and box die-lines to ensure total manufacturing feasibility.

2

Stage 2: Physical Sample Preparation & Free Laser Proofing

We prepare up to 5 free in-stock blank glasses and execute free laser logo proofing. If custom packaging is requested, box sampling is initiated simultaneously ($50–$100 refundable).

3

Stage 3: Pre-Dispatch High-Resolution Photo & 4K Video Verification

Before courier handover, our QC team shoots multi-angle studio photographs and video clips of the glassware, laser logo, and box structure for your preliminary sign-off.

4

Stage 4: International Air Express Dispatch & Golden Sample Testing

Samples are dispatched via DHL/FedEx Express (3–5 days transit to US/EU). You physically inspect glass clarity, rim mouth-feel, weight balance, and packaging unboxing quality.

5

Stage 5: Official Mass Production Sign-Off & Sample Fee Refund

Once you sign off on the physical golden sample, 100% of custom packaging sample fees are credited back to your mass production invoice, and scheduled batch manufacturing begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we receive photos and videos before the sample package is shipped?
Yes, absolutely. Our quality assurance team will send comprehensive multi-angle photos and HD inspection videos of the finished glass and laser branding to confirm visual alignment before dispatching the international parcel.
How does the 100% sample fee refund work when placing the bulk order?
When you finalize your Proforma Invoice (PI) for mass production, any custom color box ($50) or luxury wooden box ($50–$100) sample preparation fee paid will be automatically deducted as a credit line directly from your deposit balance.
