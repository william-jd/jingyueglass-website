Custom Glassware Sample Approval Process

Use this guide to understand how a custom glassware project moves from RFQ to artwork proof, sample review, packaging confirmation, production, QC and shipment. Timelines and feasibility must be confirmed by SKU, decoration method, packaging and destination.

Start a Sample ReviewPrepare RFQ Details
Quick answer: A reliable sample approval process confirms the product route, dimensions, logo or decoration proof, packaging layout, approved sample and inspection requirements before mass production. Jingyue Glass reviews these details by SKU and project scope rather than promising a fixed process for every item.

Step-by-step sample approval workflow

1. RFQ and feasibility review

Buyer sends product type, capacity, reference image, quantity, decoration idea, packaging and destination. Jingyue checks whether an available SKU, similar shape or new development route is suitable.

2. Artwork and layout proof

Logo files, print position, color and size are reviewed before sampling. Artwork proof helps avoid misunderstandings about branding placement.

3. Physical sample or reference confirmation

Depending on SKU and customization level, buyers may review a standard sample, decoration sample, packaging mockup or full pre-production sample.

4. Packaging and carton confirmation

Gift box, barcode, insert, carton mark and outer carton plan should be confirmed before mass production when retail packaging is required.

5. Production and QC alignment

The approved sample becomes the reference for appearance, decoration and packaging checks. Inspection scope should be agreed before shipment.

6. Shipment document preparation

Destination, trade term and documents are checked with the buyer so cartons, packing list and export paperwork match the order.

Common approval mistakes

Approving the logo but not the packaging
Packaging can change carton dimensions, barcode placement and retail presentation.
Assuming one sample covers every SKU
Different glass shapes may need separate decoration or pack-out confirmation.
Leaving certificate scope vague
Food-contact reports should be checked by SKU, sample and destination market.
Skipping shipment details
Trade term and destination affect quote comparison and document preparation.

Sample Approval FAQ

Can a reference photo start a project?

Yes. A reference photo is useful, but Jingyue still needs capacity, dimensions, quantity, artwork and packaging details for a practical quote and sample plan.

Should I approve packaging before mass production?

Yes, especially for retail-ready gift boxes, barcodes, inserts or carton marks. Packaging affects both presentation and logistics.

Can approved samples be used for QC reference?

Yes, approved samples and written specifications should be used as references for appearance, decoration and packaging inspection.
