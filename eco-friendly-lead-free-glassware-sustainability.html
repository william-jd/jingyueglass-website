Sustainable Eco-Friendly Glassware & ESG Manufacturing

100% lead-free crystal chemistry, clean electric furnace melting, circular recyclable lifecycles, and EU PPWR-compliant plastic-free honeycomb packaging standards.

Four Pillars of Jingyue Environmental Stewardship

1. 100% Lead-Free & Heavy-Metal Free Chemistry

Zero toxic lead (Pb) and cadmium (Cd) leaching. Formulated with inert mineral silica and potassium oxides, fully meeting FDA CPG 545.400 / 545.450 and California Prop 65 standards.

2. Clean Electric Melting Forehearths

Transitioned from traditional coal/heavy oil combustion to high-efficiency electric melting forehearths, reducing direct operational CO2 emissions by over 38% per ton of molten glass.

3. EU PPWR Plastic-Free Packaging

Zero expanded polystyrene (EPS) foam packaging. Standardized on 100% biodegradable corrugated cardboard partitions, molded paper pulp, and unbleached kraft master cartons.

4. Infinite Circular Recyclability

Glass is 100% endlessly recyclable without loss of clarity or chemical purity. In-house factory cullet is remelted at a 15–25% closed-loop circular ratio to conserve raw silica.

Sustainability & ESG FAQs

Can Jingyue provide ESG documentation for corporate supplier onboarding?
Yes. We supply comprehensive factory compliance dossiers including TÜV factory audit summaries, FDA material test reports, and non-toxic raw material SDS sheets for multinational hospitality and retail vendor registrations.
Do your custom logo inks comply with heavy metal regulations?
All our 580°C fired ceramic decals and organic screen printing inks use lead-free, cadmium-free formulations compliant with RoHS and Prop 65 lip-and-rim food contact standards.
