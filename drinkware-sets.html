FAQ
Common Questions
What is the MOQ for drinkware sets?
Our standard MOQ is 50 sets for most configurations. For fully custom OEM sets with new molds or packaging designs, the MOQ is typically 100 sets.
Can I customize the set configuration?
Yes. You can choose the number of pieces, glass styles, and packaging format. We regularly create bespoke sets for wholesalers and retailers.
Can you print our logo on the glasses and box?
Absolutely. We offer logo printing on both the glass pieces and the gift box. Send your artwork file (AI, PDF, or PNG) and we'll provide a mockup within 1 hour.
Are the sets suitable for supermarket retail?
Yes. We supply retail chains in South America including VAS Casco and Cosco. We understand retail packaging requirements such as barcode placement, shelf-ready packaging, and compliance labeling.
How do you prevent breakage during shipping?
Each piece is individually wrapped in foam and the set is packed in a reinforced outer carton. Our breakage rate for international sea freight is below 0.5%.
