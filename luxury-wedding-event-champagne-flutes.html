Frequently Asked Questions

Wedding Planner & Event Sourcing Q&A

Can we engrave individual couple names and wedding dates on both the wooden box and glasses?

Yes, absolutely! We support complete personalized customization: couple names, wedding dates, monogram logos, or custom venue branding can be laser engraved on the wooden lid, glass bowls, and metal accessories.

What is the difference between the in-stock wooden boxes (xzm132/xzm063) and the luxury painted box (xzm130)?

Models xzm132 and xzm063 feature natural burnished solid pine wood with rustic vintage bronze hardware, available from our ready in-stock inventory starting at only 24 sets. Model xzm130 features a bespoke solid wood box with multi-layer high-gloss piano lacquer or matte spray paint and polished gold hardware, requiring a 200-set custom manufacturing run.

Can we request free blank samples or custom laser proofing before confirming bulk orders?

Yes. We provide up to 5 pcs of free blank glassware samples and free digital laser artwork proofing. For physical boxed samples, sample proofing fees are 100% credited against your bulk order invoice.

How do you ensure zero glass breakage during air courier shipping to international wedding venues?

Each set is cushioned in high-density closed-cell EVA foam (precision laser die-cut to the exact millimeter dimensions of each stem and tool), encased in solid wood, and packed inside a 5-ply export master carton with corner protectors. We provide a 100% zero-breakage guarantee with free immediate replacement.
