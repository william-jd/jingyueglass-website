B2B Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Last Updated: September 26, 2026. Official privacy commitments of Wuhan Jingyueda Household Products Co., Ltd. (Jingyue Glass).

1. Overview & Scope

Wuhan Jingyueda Household Products Co., Ltd. ("Jingyue Glass", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy, commercial confidentiality, and intellectual property of international buyers, distributors, brand owners, and website visitors.

2. Commercial Information We Collect

We only collect business-relevant information necessary to evaluate procurement inquiries, quote OEM/ODM projects, prepare custom production samples, and execute international freight:

  • Contact Information: Business name, contact person, corporate email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and delivery destination.
  • Project Specifications: Target glassware SKU, requested quantity, packaging requirements, custom vector logos (AI/PDF), and delivery timelines.
  • Technical & Analytical Data: Anonymized website analytics (pages visited, device type, geographic country) to optimize website performance and user experience.

3. Non-Disclosure & IP Protection Guarantee

We treat all client vector artwork, proprietary mold drawings, packaging die-lines, and custom branding concepts as strictly confidential. We guarantee that your private label designs and trademarks will never be shared with third parties, publicly displayed without written permission, or sold to competing buyers.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to any third-party marketing brokers.
  • You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your inquiry and contact records by emailing sales@jingyueglass.com.
  • All customer records are stored on encrypted, access-controlled enterprise servers.

5. Contact Data Protection Officer

If you have questions regarding our data practices or wish to execute a custom Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (MNDA) before sharing CAD drawings, please contact our legal team at sales@jingyueglass.com or WhatsApp +86 181 8660 7062.
