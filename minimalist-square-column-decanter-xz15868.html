Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for Model XZ15868, and can we order with custom gift packaging?

How does Model XZ15868 improve aeration and spirit presentation?
The engineered crystal geometry provides optimal surface area contact, accelerating oxygenation to release volatile aromatic compounds while the tapered neck and cold-cut spout provide smooth, drip-free pouring for wines and fine spirits.
How do you package delicate decanter shapes to prevent international shipping damage?
Every decanter is encased in a custom-molded, odorless EPE pearl cotton insert that contours fully to the body and neck. Cartons are reinforced with 5-ply corrugated walls, corner protectors, and palletized with stretch film (ISTA-1A drop-test certified with <0.5% breakage).
Can we apply custom winery/distillery logos, monograms, or hand-painted gold rims?
Yes. We offer precision frosted laser engraving, 12% genuine liquid gold luster along the rim or base, sandblasted frosted relief, and custom die-cast metal adhesive crests. Digital proofs are provided within 24 hours.
What are the recommended cleaning and maintenance procedures for Model XZ15868?
Rinse with warm water and use stainless steel cleaning beads for internal residue removal. Air dry on a dedicated stand or wipe with a lint-free microfiber cloth. Hand washing is recommended to preserve acoustic crystal brilliance.
Is Model XZ15868 certified lead-free and food-safe for US and EU markets?
Yes. All crystal batches contain 0% lead and 0% cadmium, fully compliant with US FDA (21 CFR 175.300), California Prop 65, German LFGB (EC 1935/2004), and EU Directive 84/500/EEC standards. Third-party testing reports are available.
