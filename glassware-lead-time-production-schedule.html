Quick Answer

Fast reference: shipment and customization timing

The schedule below uses the timing rules confirmed by Jingyue Glass. Final dates still depend on SKU availability, artwork readiness, production queue, packaging complexity and payment confirmation.

Important: for stock orders, the countdown starts after order and payment confirmation. For custom logo, decal, UV, screen print or packaging work, timing starts after artwork, quantity, product and proofing details are confirmed.

Fastest shipping route

Confirmed in-stock glassware can usually be dispatched within 2 days after order and payment confirmation.

Fastest custom logo route

In-stock glassware with laser logo sample proofing can usually be dispatched within 1 day after artwork confirmation.

Packaging affects timing

Wooden boxes, color boxes and gift boxes require separate proofing time before bulk pack-out.

Production Workflow

Recommended B2B production schedule workflow

This sequence helps avoid delays caused by incomplete artwork, unclear packaging requirements or late sample approval.

RFQ preparation

Buyer confirms glassware type, SKU/reference image, quantity, logo requirement, packaging request, destination and trade term.

Stock and route confirmation

Jingyue checks whether the project can use in-stock glassware, laser logo, UV/screen print, high-temp decal, wooden box or custom gift box route.

Artwork and packaging proof

Logo vector file, print position, color and packaging structure are reviewed before sample proofing starts.

Sample approval gate

Buyer checks sample photos, video or physical samples for glass clarity, logo placement, box fit and overall presentation.

Bulk scheduling

Production or decoration starts after order, payment and sample/proof approval are aligned.

Inspection and shipment handoff

Final inspection, carton marking, export document preparation and carrier handoff are completed before shipment.

FAQ

Glassware lead time FAQ

How fast can stock glassware ship?

For confirmed in-stock styles, shipment can usually be arranged within 2 days after order and payment confirmation.

How fast can a laser logo sample ship?

For in-stock glassware with custom laser logo, sample dispatch can usually be arranged within 1 day after artwork and SKU details are confirmed.

How long does laser logo bulk decoration take?

For in-stock glassware with laser logo, 100 sets or less usually take 2–3 days; 500 sets or less usually take 5–7 days after confirmation.

Which decoration method takes longer?

Fired high-temp decal samples usually take 7–10 days. UV printing or screen printing usually takes 3–7 days depending on artwork and print complexity.

How long does custom packaging take?

Custom wooden box packaging usually takes 7–10 days. Custom color box or gift box packaging usually takes 10–20 days depending on dieline, artwork, insert and proofing complexity.
