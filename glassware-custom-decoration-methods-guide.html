1. Core Decoration Methods Overview

1. 580°C high-temp decal

Commercial Dishwasher Proof (1000+ Washes)

Inorganic ceramic pigments sintered directly into the glass surface at 580°C–600°C. Colors will never fade, peel, or scratch under commercial wash cycles.

  • Best for: Bars, restaurants, retail brands
  • MOQ: 500 pcs
  • Lead Time: 3-5 days sample, 15-20 days bulk

2. Precision Laser Engraving

Tactile Frosted Look (Permanent)

Computer-guided high-precision laser micro-fractures the glass surface to create crisp logos, personalized monograms, or measurement fill-lines.

  • Best for: Whiskey glasses, decanters, corporate gifting
  • MOQ: 100 pcs (Ultra-flexible)
  • Lead Time: 1-2 days sample, 7-10 days bulk

3. 24K Real Gold / Platinum Rim

Luxury Aesthetic (Hand Painted)

Genuine liquid gold or platinum solution hand-painted along the glass rim and fired at 520°C for a dazzling, reflective metallic finish.

  • Best for: High-end tasting rooms, wedding gift sets
  • MOQ: 300 pcs
  • Durability: Hand-wash recommended

4. Acid Etching & Satin Frosting

Velvety Matte Finish

Chemical acid immersion or high-pressure sandblasting creates an all-over or patterned translucent frosted surface that resists fingerprint smudges.

  • Best for: Vodka bottles, candle jars, modern tumblers
  • MOQ: 1,000 pcs
  • Durability: 100% permanent
