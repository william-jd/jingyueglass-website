1. What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for Model XZS-2606 with custom branding?

2. How does the thermal shock resistance perform for boiling and iced beverages?

Crafted from laboratory-grade high borosilicate glass 3.3, Model XZS-2606 tolerates instant thermal shocks from -20°C to +150°C without cracking. You can pour boiling espresso or tea directly into frozen or iced glassware with complete safety.

3. Are Model XZS-2606 coffee mugs commercial dishwasher and microwave safe?

Yes. The high borosilicate COE 3.3 composition is chemically inert, non-porous, and resistant to alkaline dishwasher detergents, passing 1,000+ commercial wash cycles without clouding or etching. It is also 100% microwave safe for beverage reheating.

6. What food contact and international safety standards does Model XZS-2606 meet?

100% of our production is crafted from certified lead-free, cadmium-free, BPA-free high borosilicate 3.3 glass, fully compliant with US FDA CPG 545.400 / 21 CFR 175.300, EU LFGB (EC 1935/2004), California Proposition 65, and REACH regulations.
