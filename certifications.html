⚠️ Clear Operational Scope — What We Are Not Claiming

  • Hand-Blown vs. Machine-Pressed Tolerances: Hand-blown crystal decanters and specialty art glasses possess natural artisanal characteristics. Standard height variance is ±2.5 mm and volume variance is ±3%, compared to ±1.0 mm for machine-pressed lines.
  • Food Contact Safety vs. Outer Decorative Packaging: Our food contact certificates apply directly to the glassware body and liquid-contact surfaces. Custom wooden boxes, velvet bags, and paper outer cartons are certified separately for FSC sourcing and non-toxic water-based inks upon request.
  • Dishwasher Ratings: Clear lead-free crystal is tested for 1,000+ commercial wash cycles. However, drinkware with electroplated gold/platinum rims or hand-applied metallic decals must be hand-washed to maintain luster over prolonged multi-year service.

Frequently Asked Compliance Questions

Can you provide FDA and LFGB test certificates before placing a purchase order?
Yes. We supply complete digital PDF compliance packages including third-party SGS/TÜV test reports, material safety data sheets, and ISO 9001:2015 documentation during the initial quotation stage to assist your internal compliance due diligence.
Are your crystal glasses and decanters 100% lead-free?
Yes. All Jingyue crystal stemware, whiskey glasses, and wine decanters are manufactured from ultra-clear barium/potassium acoustic crystal or high-borosilicate glass. Leachable lead (Pb) and cadmium (Cd) are non-detectable under US FDA CPG 7117.06/07 and EU LFGB testing.
Can our company arrange a third-party inspection (SGS, Bureau Veritas, Intertek)?
Absolutely. We welcome third-party quality control inspections at our factory prior to container loading. Our standard internal quality baseline strictly follows ISO 2859-1 Level II AQL 1.0/2.5 standards.
Do you provide custom compliance documentation for regional customs clearance?
Yes. We provide complete export documentation tailored to destination markets, including Certificate of Origin (CO, Form A/E/F), Fumigation Certificates for solid wood packaging, and EU PPWR-compliant packaging declarations.
