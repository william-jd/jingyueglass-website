Whiskey glass sourcing FAQ

Can I start with a mixed whiskey-glass order?

For available standard SKUs, a low-volume mixed test order may start from 30 pieces per SKU. Final quantities depend on the selected item, stock status, packaging and destination.

Can you add a logo to whiskey glasses?

Yes. Decoration options can include laser engraving, screen printing, decal application and other finishes. The suitable process and MOQ are confirmed after reviewing the artwork, glass style and quantity.

What should I send for a quotation?

Send the preferred glass style or reference image, estimated quantity, logo or packaging request, destination country and target delivery date. Jingyue Glass can then confirm the suitable SKU and quotation route.

Need help selecting whiskey glasses for your market?

Send your target style, quantity and branding requirement by WhatsApp. We will confirm the available options and the correct order route.
