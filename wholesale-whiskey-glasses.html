Complete Whiskey Glasses Sourcing & Buying Guide

Key technical considerations when importing commercial barware from Chinese manufacturers.

1. Weighted Base (Heavy Sham) vs. Lightweight Glassware

A substantial weighted base (typically 15mm–22mm thick) lowers the center of gravity, significantly reducing accidental tip-overs in busy bar and restaurant environments. In Jingyue's automated pressing process, controlled cooling in 45-meter annealing lehrs eliminates internal thermal stress, ensuring crystal clarity without vacuum bubbles or distortion.

2. Food-Contact Compliance: Lead-Free Crystal Standards

All Jingyue whiskey glasses are manufactured from premium high-white soda-lime or potassium-based lead-free crystal glass. Independent lab test reports (SGS / STQ) confirm zero detectable lead (Pb) and cadmium (Cd) leaching, fully compliant with FDA CPG 7117.06/07, California Proposition 65, and European Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004.

3. Laser Engraving vs. High-Fire Ceramic Decal Branding

  • Fiber Laser Engraving: Ideal for low-MOQ runs (from 48 pcs), corporate monograms, and rapid prototyping. Delivers crisp, frosted, permanent etching in 3–5 working days.
  • 580°C Vitrified Ceramic Decal: Pigments permanently fuse into the molten glass glaze at 580°C. Recommended for colorful multi-tone logos and commercial hospitality requiring 1,000+ industrial dishwashing cycles.

4. Breakage Prevention: ISTA-1A Certified Packaging

Transit breakage is the primary operational risk in glassware imports. We utilize 5-layer double-wall corrugated shipping cartons (BC flute) and custom-cut odorless EPE (Expanded Polyethylene) foam instead of smelly recycled EVA foam. Every packaging configuration passes strict 1.2-meter drop tests prior to container loading.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about ordering, sampling, and customization.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for custom whiskey glasses?
For standard stock models with laser logo engraving, our MOQ is only 48 pieces (1 master carton) with a 3–5 day lead time. For full custom ceramic decal firing (580°C) or bespoke color gift box packaging, the MOQ is 500–1,000 pieces. Completely new OEM mould tooling starts at 3,000–5,000 pieces.
Can I mix different whiskey glass models in one container (FCL)?
Yes, we specialize in mixed container consolidation. You can combine whiskey glasses, wine glasses, decanters, and cocktail tumblers in a single 20GP or 40HQ container, optimizing freight costs for wholesale distributors.
How does your custom mould fee refund policy work?
When you develop a private OEM mould with Jingyue Glass, 100% of the mould tooling fee is credited back to your account once your cumulative order volume reaches 30,000 units.
Are the logo prints and gold rims dishwasher safe?
Our 580°C high-temperature ceramic decals are 100% dishwasher safe and tested for over 1,000 commercial cycles. Hand-painted real gold rims and electroplated coatings should be washed with mild detergents or gentle cycles to maintain shine.
