Wholesale Whiskey Glass FAQ

What is the MOQ for custom laser branding vs decal printing?
Laser engraving starts at only 48 pcs with a 1–3 working day turnaround. High-temperature 580°C high-temp decal require 500 pcs (7–10 days turnaround) to ensure 1,000+ cycle commercial dishwasher durability.
Can we request free samples to inspect glass clarity and weight?
Yes! We provide up to 5 pcs of free in-stock blank glasses and free laser engraving proofing. Buyer only covers international express courier freight (DHL/FedEx).
How are custom boxes sampled and charged?
Custom color boxes and burnt pine wood gift boxes are sampled for ~$50 USD (7-15 days). High-gloss lacquer boxes are ~$100 USD. All sample packaging fees are 100% credited back against your bulk order.
What certifications do your whiskey glasses meet?
All glasses are certified 100% lead-free crystal, fully compliant with FDA CPG 545.400, LFGB, and California Proposition 65 heavy metal leaching standards.
