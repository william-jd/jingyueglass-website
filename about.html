About Jingyue Glass

Wuhan Jingyueda Household Products Co., Ltd. is a factory-direct glassware supplier and wholesale partner based in Wuhan, Hubei, China. We help importers, distributors, gift companies and retail buyers source drinkware, wine glasses, decanters, beer glasses, whiskey sets and custom packaging with clearer communication and reliable order support.

Company Profile
