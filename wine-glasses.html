Wholesale Wine Glasses FAQ

What is the MOQ for wholesale custom wine glasses from Jingyue Glass?
For standard in-stock styles with custom laser branding, MOQ starts at only 48 pcs (1-3 working day sample turnaround). For 580°C high-temp decal or custom packaging boxes, MOQ is 500 pcs.
Are your crystal wine glasses lead-free and commercial dishwasher safe?
Yes, 100% of our wine glasses are manufactured from lead-free crystal or high-clarity soda lime glass compliant with FDA CPG 545.400 and California Prop 65. Laser cold-cut rims withstand 1,000+ commercial wash cycles.
