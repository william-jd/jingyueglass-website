Commercial-Grade Crystal Engineering Standards

Every Jingyue stemware SKU is precision crafted to withstand heavy hospitality rotation while maintaining crystal acoustic ring and optical brilliance.

✨

Laser Cold-Cut Rim (1.0–1.2mm)

Seamless rim cut with high-precision CO2 laser and flame polished. Delivers smooth wine delivery to palate with zero rolled bead and chip resistance.

🍷

Seamless Pulled Stem

Monobloc one-piece pulled stem technology eliminates seam lines between bowl, stem, and foot. Provides superior torsional strength under commercial wiping.

🛡️

1,000+ Cycle Dishwasher Safe

High-purity potassium crystal formulation resists alkaline clouding and chemical corrosion in commercial dishwashers up to 65°C.

🍾

Laser Nucleation Points

Micro-etched nucleation dots at the bottom of champagne flutes ensure a continuous, elegant stream of fine bubbles for sparkling wines.

Wholesale Wine Glasses FAQ

What is the MOQ for wholesale custom wine glasses from Jingyue Glass?
For standard in-stock styles with custom laser branding, MOQ starts at only 48 pcs (1-3 working day sample turnaround). For 580°C high-temp decal or custom packaging boxes, MOQ is 500 pcs.
Are your crystal wine glasses lead-free and commercial dishwasher safe?
Yes, 100% of our wine glasses are manufactured from lead-free crystal or high-clarity soda lime glass compliant with FDA CPG 545.400 and California Prop 65. Laser cold-cut rims withstand 1,000+ commercial wash cycles.
Can we request free samples before ordering in bulk?
Yes, we provide up to 5 pcs of free in-stock blank glasses and free laser engraving proofing. Buyer only covers international express courier freight (DHL/FedEx).
Can you customize tasting pour lines for winery tasting rooms?
Yes, we offer calibrated 50ml, 100ml, and 150ml laser-etched pour lines, bottom-stem brand stamping, and 24K gold/platinum rim detailing for tasting rooms and sommelier wine clubs.
