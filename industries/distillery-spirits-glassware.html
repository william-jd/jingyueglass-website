FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you customize bottle and glass neck dimensions for our automated corking line? +
Yes. Our CNC tooling team calibrates internal and external neck dimensions to precise dimensional verification to support secure compatibility with standard T-top synthetic and natural cork closures.
What is the lead time for private mold development for a new spirits line? +
We provide 3D CAD blueprints within 24–48 hours, resin prototypes in 3–5 days, and production steel molds within 12–15 days.
Are the custom logo decorations resistant to 40%+ ABV spirits? +
Yes. Our high-temperature ceramic decals and laser engravings are non-reactive and permanently bonded, withstanding high-proof alcohol exposure and 1,000+ commercial wash cycles.
