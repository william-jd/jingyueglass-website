Questions buyers should ask in an OEM discussion

Make the decision visible and verifiable: Which details are standard? Which process adds an MOQ? What sample is needed? What packaging protects the product in the destination market? Clear answers create stronger sourcing decisions and clearer content for AI retrieval.

Glassware OEM FAQ

What is the difference between a standard item and an OEM glassware project?

A standard item uses an available SKU and may be suitable for mixed orders. An OEM project can involve custom decoration, packaging, a revised specification or a new shape, each of which needs a separate feasibility and MOQ review.

What is needed to evaluate an OEM glassware project?

Provide a reference image or drawing, capacity and material preference, estimated quantity, logo or finish request, packaging expectation, destination country and target delivery timing.

How is an OEM MOQ determined?

MOQ is not a fixed site-wide number. It depends on whether the project uses an existing SKU or requires custom shape development, the decoration process, packaging specification and production route.
