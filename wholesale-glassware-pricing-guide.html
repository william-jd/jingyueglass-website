3. Frequently Asked Questions on Wholesale Pricing

What payment terms are accepted for wholesale glassware orders?

Standard terms are 30% T/T deposit upon purchase order confirmation and 70% balance paid against the copy of the Bill of Lading (B/L). We also accept Alibaba Trade Assurance, L/C at sight for container orders, and PayPal for sample courier fees.

Do you provide DDP shipping rates to US/European warehouses or Amazon FBA?

Yes. In addition to FOB Wuhan/Shanghai and CIF destination port, we offer door-to-door DDP air and sea shipping directly to commercial warehouses, restaurant venues, and Amazon fulfillment centers (e.g. ONT8, FTW1, CLT2) with all customs clearance, duties, and Section 301 tariffs included.

How do you guarantee zero breakage during ocean container transit?

All bulk orders adhere to ISTA-1A drop test standards. We utilize heavy-duty 5-ply export double-wall cartons, custom EPE/EVA protective foam inserts, corner protectors, and pallet shrink-wrapping. We back every order with a 30-Day Zero-Risk Breakage Replacement Guarantee.
