1. Overview: Transparent B2B Contracting

Clear, legally compliant commercial terms are the cornerstone of smooth cross-border glassware procurement. A well-structured Proforma Invoice (PI) and Sales Confirmation eliminates misunderstandings regarding dimensional tolerances, packaging specifications, shipping terms, and payment milestones.

2. Key Elements Required in a Standard Glassware Proforma Invoice (PI)

Every commercial PI issued by Jingyue Glass incorporates the following contractual clauses:

1. Legal Parties & Banking Details

Full corporate legal names, registration numbers, verified beneficiary bank details (SWIFT/IBAN), and authorized signatory stamps.

2. Technical Product Specifications

Model SKU numbers, glass material composition (Lead-free crystal / High-borosilicate), dimensions (height, diameter, capacity), and decoration methods.

3. Packaging & Packing Details

Inner box configuration (color gift box, drop-tested mailer box, or bulk divider pack), master carton dimensions, gross/net weights, and pallet specs.

4. Incoterms 2020 & Port of Loading

FOB (Wuhan / Shanghai / Ningbo / Qingdao), CIF (Named Destination Port), or DDP (Delivered Duty Paid to client warehouse).

5. Production Lead Time & Schedule

Clear timeline: 20–35 days from receipt of 30% deposit and pre-production golden sample physical sign-off.

6. Inspection Standard & Warranty

AQL 2.5 Major / 4.0 Minor acceptance standards under ISO 2859-1, backed by our 30-Day Transit Breakage Replacement Guarantee.

30-Day Transit Breakage Protection Policy

We stand firmly behind our 1.2M drop-tested export packaging. If transit breakage exceeds the standard 1.5% maritime tolerance, buyers simply provide unsealing photos within 30 days of container arrival. We issue immediate credit notes or dispatch replacement stock at our cost.

Frequently Asked Questions on B2B Glassware Contracts

Can we pay balance payments after container destination arrival?
For standard orders, balance payments are due upon presentation of the Bill of Lading (B/L) copy and pre-shipment QC pass report. Open Account (OA) credit terms can be evaluated for contracted repeat clients via Sinosure credit insurance.
Do you provide Certificate of Origin (Form A, Form E, RCEP)?
Yes. We supply officially legalized Certificates of Origin (CO), Form E (for ASEAN), and RCEP certificates to help importers minimize destination import customs duties.
