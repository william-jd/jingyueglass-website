Cigar Lounge Procurement FAQs

What is the standard packaging difference between cigar glasses and regular whiskey tumblers?
Our XZ5411-X Cigar Rest Tumblers are individually packaged in protective Single Kraft Gift Boxes (1 pc/box), and XZ0107-2D Rolling Rocks Tumblers come in individual Single Color Gift Boxes (1 pc/box) with solid wood coasters. How fast can we receive branded laser-engraved samples for our club?
Laser engraved and UV printed samples are completed in 1–3 working days. Physical samples are dispatched via DHL/FedEx Express with 3–5 days transit time directly to your lounge.
