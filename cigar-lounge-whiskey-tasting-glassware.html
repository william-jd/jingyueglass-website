Cigar Lounge Procurement FAQs

What is the standard packaging difference between cigar glasses and regular whiskey tumblers?
Our Cigar Rest Tumblers are individually packaged in protective Single Kraft Gift Boxes (1 pc/box), and Rolling Rocks Tumblers come in individual Single Color Gift Boxes (1 pc/box), making them immediately ready for single-unit retail or VIP gifting. Standard Vintage Diamond Tumblers are packed in commercial 6-pack color boxes with internal cardboard dividers (6 pcs/box) to maximize packing efficiency and prevent transit damage.
How fast can we receive branded laser-engraved samples for our club?
Laser engraved and UV printed samples are completed in 1–3 working days. Physical samples are dispatched via DHL/FedEx Express with 3–5 days transit time directly to your lounge.
