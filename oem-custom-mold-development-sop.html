1. Overview: Bespoke Glassware Tooling Engineering

Custom glassware development requires a precise balance between aesthetic design vision, optical clarity, structural strength, and high-speed automated production yield. At Jingyue Glass, our dedicated mold tooling center combines CNC 5-axis machining with rapid prototyping to shorten development cycles from months to under 25 days.

2. The 5-Stage Tooling & Sampling SOP

Day 01 – 02

1. 3D CAD & DFM Feasibility

We analyze customer CAD files (.STEP, .IGES, .DWG) or reference sketches to calculate volume capacity, draft angles (> 2.5°), wall thickness uniformity (1.2–3.5mm), and mold parting line locations.

Day 03 – 05

2. 1:1 SLA Resin Prototyping

High-precision 3D resin printing creates a 1:1 physical sample to verify tactile ergonomics, hand grip, rim diameter, visual proportions, and packaging insert fit before cutting metal.

Day 06 – 15

3. Rapid Sample Mold Tooling

Fabrication of a single-cavity prototype mold in aerospace-grade aluminum or fine-grain cast iron. We blow 6–12 pieces of genuine lead-free crystal glass for customer liquid tasting and drop testing.

Day 16 – 25

4. Mass Production Steel Tooling

Upon sample sign-off, we machine full multi-cavity hardened alloy steel production molds equipped with thermal expansion compensation and polished optical cavity chrome plating.

Frequently Asked Questions on Custom Glass Molds

Can you work from a physical reference glass or rough napkin sketch?
Yes. You can ship us an existing sample glass or provide 2D dimensioned sketches. Our in-house CAD engineers will generate full 3D parametric STEP files and photorealistic 3D renderings within 48 hours.
What custom branding techniques can be integrated into the mold?
We can integrate raised embossed logos on the bottom base, debossed stem branding, optic ribbed surface patterns, facet cuts, and honeycomb textures directly into the mold cavity.
