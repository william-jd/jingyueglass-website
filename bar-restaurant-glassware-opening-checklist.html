2. Dishwasher Durability & Thermal Shock Mitigation

Commercial underbar glasswashers (such as Hobart or Winterhalter) subject glass to rapid temperature shifts (65°C wash to 85°C rinse). Our commercial glassware delivers:

  • 1,000+ Wash Cycle Resistance: Tested without clouding, etching, or surface micro-cracking.
  • Annealed Thermal Stress Relief: 580°C continuous lehr annealing removes internal tension points.
  • Laser Cold-Cut Fire-Polished Rims: Replaces fragile beaded rims with durable, ultra-smooth edges that resist chipping by over 40%.
