Frequently Asked Questions

E-Commerce Sourcing & FBA Logistics FAQ

Does Jingyue Glass packaging pass Amazon FBA 1.2-meter drop test standards?

Yes. All our e-commerce gift boxes and master export cartons are engineered with CNC-molded odorless EPE pearl cotton inserts and 5-layer corrugated cardboard, tested to pass ISTA-1A 1.2M multi-angle drop protocols with zero breakage.

Do you handle FNSKU barcode labeling and direct Amazon FBA delivery?

Yes. We apply scannable FNSKU item barcodes, polybag warning labels, and carton box labels per Amazon's exact fulfillment guidelines, and coordinate direct DDP air and sea freight delivery to Amazon warehouses in the US, Europe, and Australia.

What is the MOQ for private label glassware with custom packaging?

MOQ starts from only 100–200 sets (100 sets for flagship wooden gift sets XZM015 and XZM108-A, 200 sets for decanters and cocktail smoker kits), allowing sellers to test new niches with minimal initial risk.

How long does sample proofing take and is the fee refundable?

Physical pre-production samples with custom laser logos and branded gift packaging are dispatched via DHL/FedEx in 3–5 working days. The sample fee is 100% credited back upon placing the bulk production order.
