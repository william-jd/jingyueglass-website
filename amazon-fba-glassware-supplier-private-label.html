E-Commerce Sourcing FAQs

Frequently Asked Amazon Seller Questions

Does Jingyue Glass packaging pass Amazon FBA 1.2-meter drop test standards?

Yes, all our e-commerce gift boxes and master cartons are tested according to ISTA-1A drop protocols (1 corner, 3 edges, 6 faces from 1.2 meters) using high-density CNC molded EVA foam and 5-layer corrugated export cartons to ensure zero transit breakage.

Do you provide Amazon FBA prep, FNSKU labeling, and DDP shipping to Amazon warehouses?

Yes, we handle complete Amazon FBA prep including FNSKU item barcode labeling, suffocation warning polybags, outer carton box labels, and direct DDP air/sea freight delivery to Amazon fulfillment centers in the US, Europe, and Australia.

What is the MOQ for private label glassware with custom packaging?

For standard glassware with custom logo laser engraving and customized branded color boxes, MOQ starts from only 200–300 sets (200 sets for decanter and smoker sets, 300 sets for double-wall mugs and tumblers), enabling e-commerce sellers to test new product niches with minimal initial capital risk.
