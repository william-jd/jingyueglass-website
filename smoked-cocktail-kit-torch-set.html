Frequently Asked Questions

Are the wood chips and chimney food-contact safe?
Yes. Our chimney and wood chips are produced from 100% natural kiln-dried hardwoods without chemical binders, complying with US FDA and California Proposition 65 food-contact safety standards.
Can we ship torches filled with butane gas?
Due to international air and ocean cargo hazardous material regulations, blowtorches are shipped empty without butane gas. Buyers or end recipients fill standard butane fuel locally.
