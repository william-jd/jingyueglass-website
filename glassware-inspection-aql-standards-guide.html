1. Executive Summary & Inspection Scope

In international B2B glassware procurement, defect disputes frequently arise from ambiguous cosmetic vs functional standards. Jingyue Glass implements the ISO 2859-1 (ANSI/ASQ Z1.4) Normal Single Sampling Plan (General Inspection Level II) across all machine-blown and hand-crafted crystal glassware production lines.

Our quality control protocol defines rigorous boundaries across visual, dimensional, thermal, and chemical criteria, ensuring full compliance with US FDA CPG 545.400 / 545.450 food contact safety and EU Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004.

2. Three-Tier Glassware Defect Classification Matrix

Defects are categorized based on consumer safety hazards, brand integrity risks, and structural usability:

Critical Defects (AQL 0.0)

Max Allowed: 0 pcs

Defects that pose immediate consumer injury risks or violate mandatory regulatory compliance laws:

  • Sharp Burrs & Glass Chipping: Any sharp glass protrusion along the laser-cut rim, stem junction, or base rim.
  • Cracks & Fractures: Through-cracks, surface micro-fissures, or deep stress checks in the glass wall.
  • Heavy Lead / Cadmium Leaching: Exceeding FDA limits (< 0.5 ppm for drinking vessels).
  • Un-annealed Internal Stress: Residual stress exceeding ASTM C148 Temper Grade 3, risking spontaneous thermal bursting.
  • Foreign Metal Inclusions: Embedded ferrous particles or sharp refractory debris inside the glass matrix.

Major Defects (AQL 2.5)

Max Allowed: AQL 2.5

Functional defects or prominent visual flaws that affect product salability and commercial use:

  • Rim Ovality / Out-of-Round: Mouth diameter variance > 0.8mm on stemmed wine glasses.
  • Wobble / Rocking Base: Base flatness deviation > 0.5mm causing tabletop instability.
  • Dense Bubble Clusters: Concentrated open bubbles (> 1.5mm diameter) located in the primary visual zone (Zone A).
  • Heavy Mold Seam Marks: Excessive tactile parting line ridge > 0.15mm along the stem or body.
  • Capacity Variance: Fluid volume deviation exceeding ±3% of nominal capacity.
  • Logo / Decal Misalignment: Position shift > 2.0mm or decal peeling after 100 industrial wash cycles.

Minor Defects (AQL 4.0)

Max Allowed: AQL 4.0

Minor cosmetic blemishes that do not impair structural integrity or primary product usability:

  • Isolated Micro-Bubbles: Single isolated closed seeds (< 1.0mm) in lower body (Zone B/C) with > 30mm spacing.
  • Faint Chill Lines (Shear Marks): Slight optical cooling waves on heavy base tumblers visible only under angled light.
  • Minor Outer Box Scuffs: Non-punctured master carton surface rubbing resulting from transit vibrations.
  • Slight Color Tint Variance: Minor batch-to-batch optical clarity difference within ΔE < 1.0 color space.

4. In-House Factory QC Testing & Validation SOPs

01
Polariscope Annealing Inspection

Every batch passes polariscope birefringence testing to guarantee complete lehr thermal stress relief (ASTM C148 Temper Grade 1–2).

02
ΔT Thermal Shock Cycle Test

Submerging samples from 60°C hot water directly into 18°C cold water (ΔT ≥ 42°C) with zero fracture or micro-cracking.

03
Commercial Dishwasher Cycle

Testing laser engraving and 580°C high-temp decal through 500–1,000 commercial wash cycles without fading or clouding.

04
ISTA-1A 1.2M Drop Testing

Full 1-corner, 3-edge, 6-face free fall drop test on finished export master cartons to guarantee zero courier and sea transit breakage.

Frequently Asked Questions on Glassware QC Standards

What is the difference between visual Zone A and Zone B on wine glasses?
Zone A includes the top rim and upper 50% of the drinking bowl, where visual clarity is most critical. Zone B covers the lower bowl and stem, while Zone C represents the bottom base. Stricter bubble and streak tolerances apply to Zone A.
What happens if a pre-shipment inspection fails the AQL 2.5 threshold?
If major defects exceed the acceptance number, the entire batch is halted, 100% sorted by our dedicated QC re-work team, defective items replaced, and re-inspected at factory expense before container loading.
Do you provide inspection certificates with every shipment?
Yes. Every outgoing shipment is accompanied by an in-house Certificate of Analysis (COA), Batch Inspection Report with actual sample photos, and dimensional measurement data sheets.
