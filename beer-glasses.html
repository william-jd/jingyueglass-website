FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What is the MOQ for branded beer mugs?
For standard in-stock mugs with logo printing, the MOQ is just 30 pcs. For custom-shaped molds, the MOQ is usually 500 pcs.
Are your beer glasses dishwasher safe?
Yes, all our standard beer glasses and mugs are made from soda-lime glass which is commercial dishwasher safe. For gold-rimmed or delicate decal designs, we recommend hand washing.
Can you provide custom packaging for retail?
Absolutely. We offer 4-color gift boxes, bulk cartons, and specialized protective packaging for e-commerce and retail supermarkets.
Do you have certification for EU and US markets?
Yes, our glassware is FDA and LFGB certified. We undergo regular testing for lead and cadmium to ensure 100% safety.
How do you handle shipping to avoid breakage?
We use high-density foam, honeycomb cardboard, and reinforced outer cartons. Our breakage rate for international sea freight is below 0.5%. We provide insurance for all shipments.
