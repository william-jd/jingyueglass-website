FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What is the MOQ for branded beer mugs?
MOQ depends on the selected SKU, current availability, logo process, artwork and packaging route. Share the SKU, quantity and logo file so Jingyue Glass can confirm the practical minimum before quotation.
Are your beer glasses dishwasher safe?
Yes, all our standard beer glasses and mugs are made from soda-lime glass which is commercial dishwasher safe. For gold-rimmed or delicate decal designs, we recommend hand washing.
Can you provide custom packaging for retail?
Custom packaging can be reviewed for retail, ecommerce or bar-merchandise programs. Box type, dividers, labels and carton protection should be confirmed with the selected glass SKU and quantity.
Do you have certification for EU and US markets?
Test reports and certification scope should be confirmed by SKU and destination market. Ask our team for the applicable FDA/LFGB documentation during the quotation stage.
How do you handle shipping to avoid breakage?
Packaging is selected according to glass shape, carton quantity and shipping route. Dividers, reinforced cartons or other protective options can be reviewed for export orders.
Which beer glass styles work best for brewery logos?
Straight-sided pub glasses and handled mugs usually provide easier logo areas, while stemmed goblets, pilsner glasses and novelty mugs should be checked by artwork size and decoration method.
What should a brewery or bar send for a custom beer glass quote?
Send the target SKU or glass style, quantity, logo artwork, desired logo size and position, packaging requirement and destination. This makes the quote comparable and avoids missing decoration or carton costs.
