Model XZ70-3 is a cornerstone of traditional pub drinkware, combining an expansive 500ml capacity with a robust 580g heavy base that virtually eliminates accidental tip-overs. The ergonomic handle keeps warm hands away from chilled draught beer, preserving cold serving temperatures.

Frequently Asked Questions · Model XZ70-3

What is the weight and stability rating of Model XZ70-3?

XZ70-3 weighs approximately 580g with a 98.1mm base diameter, providing commercial-grade anti-topple stability on slick bar counters.

Can we engrave our pub logo on the bottom or front of the mug?

Yes. We offer precision CO2 laser engraving on the front body and custom embossed bottom mold tooling for large volume orders.

What is the packaging specification for ocean freight?

Packed 24 pcs per 5-ply reinforced master carton with individual honeycomb cell dividers, drop-tested to ISTA-1A safety standards.

Is XZ70-3 suitable for freezing prior to beer service?

Yes. The fully annealed soda-lime glass withstands standard freezer chilling down to -18°C when dried thoroughly prior to freezing.
