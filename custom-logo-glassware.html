Common glassware decoration routes

The right treatment is selected for the artwork, glass surface, intended use and order scale—not simply for visual effect.

Laser engraving

A permanent, precise option for selected glass surfaces. It is useful when a clean, understated mark is preferred and can be assessed from the artwork and product.

Print and decal options

Screen printing and decal application support many logo layouts. Artwork colors, printable area and durability expectations guide the recommendation.

Decorative finishes

Color spray, frosting, gold detailing, electroplating, hand painting and UV printing can be considered when the design and product route allow.

Custom logo glassware FAQ

Which logo processes are available for glassware?

Available options may include laser engraving, screen printing, decal application, color spray, frosting, gold detailing, electroplating, hand painting and UV printing. The best method depends on the glass, artwork, durability goal and quantity.

What is the MOQ for custom logo glassware?

There is no single custom-logo MOQ. It is confirmed after the standard or custom glass SKU, decoration process, artwork, packaging and quantity are reviewed.

What files are needed for a logo project?

Send a vector logo when available, such as AI, PDF or SVG, plus Pantone or color references, desired logo location, approximate quantity and a reference image of the glass or package.

Have artwork ready?

Send it with the glass style and approximate quantity. We will help identify a practical decoration and packaging route.
