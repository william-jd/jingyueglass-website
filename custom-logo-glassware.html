Frequently Asked Questions on Custom Logo Glassware

What are the MOQs and wash guidelines for spray color, electroplating, and silk screen printing?

Silk Screen Printing: Low MOQ of 200 pcs. It is dishwasher safe and ideal for 1-2 solid color logos.
Spray Color Coating: MOQ is 600 pcs. Hand-wash is recommended (not harsh commercial dishwashers). Tiered bulk discounts apply for larger volumes.
Electroplating (Mirror metallic): MOQ is 600 pcs. Hand-wash recommended to maintain metallic luster. Volume tiered pricing applies.

Which file formats are required for artwork submission?

We recommend vector formats (AI, EPS, SVG, or vector PDF) with all text converted to curves/outlines. Please include Pantone Solid Coated (PMS) color references for accurate color matching on spray, decal, and silk printing.

Can I order pre-production samples before mass manufacturing?

Yes. Digital DFM visual mockups are provided within 24 hours free of charge. Physical golden pre-production samples with custom logo / finish are prepared and shipped via express courier within 3–7 business days upon confirmation.

How are fragile decorated glasses packaged for international freight?

We provide drop-test certified packaging solutions including high-density custom-molded EPE foam cradles, individual white/color boxes, double-wall master export cartons, and fumigated palletization with corner guards for zero-breakage delivery.
