FAQ
Common Questions
What is the MOQ for gift sets?
Our MOQ is just 20 sets for most standard configurations. This is one of the lowest in the industry and makes it easy for small importers and e-commerce sellers to get started.
Can I put my own logo on the gift box and glasses?
Yes. We offer full branding: 4-color printed gift box, logo on glasses (screen print, engraving, or decal), and custom ribbon or tissue paper inside the box.
Are your gift sets suitable for Amazon or Etsy selling?
Absolutely. We design packaging to meet e-commerce drop-test requirements. Many of our clients successfully sell our gift sets on Amazon US, Amazon EU, and Etsy.
How do you prevent breakage during shipping?
Each piece is individually wrapped in foam inside the gift box, and the outer carton uses reinforced corrugated cardboard. Our international breakage rate is below 0.5%.
Can I mix different gift set styles in one order?
Yes. You can mix wine sets, whiskey sets, and champagne sets in one shipment, with a minimum of 20 sets per style.
