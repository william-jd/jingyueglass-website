Frequently Asked Questions · Model XZJ0132

What is the MOQ for Model XZJ0132, and can we start with a small trial order?

The standard MOQ is 48 pcs (one master carton). For importers, hotels and distributors testing a new line, this lets you validate shelf presentation and buyer response before committing to full 20GP/40HQ container volumes. Larger quantities unlock better per-unit pricing.

Can we add our winery, hotel or private-label logo, and how do we approve it?

Yes. We support laser engraving, 580°C high-temperature decals, silk screen and gold-trim branding from 48 pcs. We provide a digital proof in 1–3 days, then a physical golden sample so you can confirm artwork position, color reference and decoration quality against a signed specification before mass production starts.

How do you prevent breakage during ocean freight to our market?

Each glass is individually wrapped and separated with cross-dividers inside 5-ply export master cartons. Gift orders add custom-cut odorless EPE pearl cotton inserts, foam corner blocks and a protective top layer, and cartons are palletized and stretch-wrapped. This ISTA-1A-oriented packing keeps transit breakage low for long-haul container shipping.

What is the lead time for samples and bulk production?

Samples are available in 1–3 working days so your team can inspect balance, rim feel and clarity first-hand. After sample approval and deposit, bulk production lead time is 7–15 working days depending on quantity and the decoration method chosen.

Is Model XZJ0132 food-safe and compliant for the US and EU markets?

Yes. The glass is lead-free and tested to LFGB, FDA, CE and California Prop 65 standards, so it clears food-contact and heavy-metal requirements for North American and European importers, restaurant groups and retail buyers. Inspection reports are available on request.

Can we combine Model XZJ0132 with decanters and other glassware in one container?

Absolutely. As a factory-direct supplier we consolidate Model XZJ0132 with decanters, whiskey tumblers, gift sets and barware accessories in a single FCL/LCL mixed-SKU shipment under FOB, CIF or DDP terms — maximizing container space and reducing freight cost and inventory risk for distributors and private labels.
