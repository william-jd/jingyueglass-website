Frequently Asked Questions

Corporate Buyer Procurement Q&A

Can we engrave different individual employee names or just one company logo?

We support both! We can engrave a unified corporate logo across the batch, or execute variable data laser engraving (individual recipient names + logo) for VIP executive gifts upon receiving your name list in Excel.

Are the natural granite chilling stones safe and certified for drinking?

Natural granite and basalt accessory options can be reviewed for the selected set. Material and food-contact documentation are confirmed against the exact accessory included in the quotation.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) and sample policy?

MOQ, sample fees and any credit policy are confirmed in the itemized quotation after the set configuration, artwork, quantity and destination are reviewed.

How is the gift set prepared for international delivery?

Each confirmed configuration uses an odorless EPE pearl cotton insert to help cushion and separate the contents. The outer packing method and any transit-testing requirements are confirmed for the selected product, quantity and delivery route.
