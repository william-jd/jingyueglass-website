Frequently Asked Questions

Corporate Buyer Procurement Q&A

Can we engrave different individual employee names or just one company logo?

We support both! We can engrave a unified corporate logo across the batch, or execute variable data laser engraving (individual recipient names + logo) for VIP executive gifts upon receiving your name list in Excel.

Are the natural granite chilling stones safe and certified for drinking?

Yes. Our stones are cut from 100% natural solid granite and basalt, rounded to prevent glass scratching, and thoroughly tumbled. They are non-porous, odorless, and certified under FDA 21 CFR 175.300 and EU LFGB food-contact safety standards.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) and sample policy?

Our MOQ is only 24 sets for both XZM4008-1 and XZM015 with custom laser branding. For physical sample testing, sample fees are 100% refundable and credited against your bulk order invoice.

How do you ensure zero breakage during international courier or Amazon FBA delivery?

Each set is cushioned in high-density closed-cell EVA foam (recessed cavities for each individual glass and stone), encased in a 1,200g rigid box/solid pine wood, and packed inside a 5-ply export master carton with corner protectors. Breakage rate is guaranteed under 0.5% with free replacement coverage.
