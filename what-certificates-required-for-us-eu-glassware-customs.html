Glassware Customs Clearance: Essential Testing & Certificates for US (FDA / Prop 65) and EU (LFGB)

Published: October 25, 2026 · 8 min read · By Jingyue Glass Compliance & QA Team
Quick Compliance Summary:

Glassware imported into the United States must comply with FDA CPG 7117.06/07 food-contact safety criteria (ASTM C738 leach testing for lead and cadmium) and California Proposition 65 thresholds. Shipments to the European Union require compliance with Framework Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004 and German LFGB Section 30/31. All shipments must declare Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) Code 7013.37 for commercial drinking glasses.

1. Regulatory Compliance Matrix for US & EU Imports

Regulatory Standard Jurisdiction Testing Method & Parameter Pass / Compliance Threshold
FDA CPG 7117.06 / 7117.07 United States (Federal) ASTM C738 acid leach testing (24h 4% acetic acid) Lead (Pb) < 0.5 – 1.5 ppm, Cadmium (Cd) < 0.5 ppm depending on vessel volume
California Proposition 65 California, USA Total lead/cadmium content & exterior lip-rim leach Pb < 200 ppm total (or clear warning label statement)
EC Regulation 1935/2004 European Union Overall migration & specific heavy metal transfer Food contact safe; no organoleptic alteration of beverage
German LFGB (§ 30 & 31) Germany / EU Strict Heavy metal release & sensory taste/odor evaluation Non-detectable heavy metal leaching; zero chemical odor

2. Critical Customs Clearance Documentation Checklist

  • Commercial Invoice & Packing List: Itemized SKU descriptions, net/gross weights, carton dimensions, and accurate HS Code (7013.37.00 for lead-free drinking glasses; 7013.99.00 for decanters).
  • Third-Party Laboratory Test Reports: Accredited test certificates from ISO 17025 certified labs (such as SGS, TÜV, or STQ) issued within valid audit cycles.
  • FDA Prior Notice Confirmation: Required for commercial container entry into US ports prior to vessel docking.
  • Packaging Material Declarations: Declaration of solid wood heat treatment (ISPM 15 stamp for solid pine boxes) and odorless EPE pearl cotton insert compliance with EU PPWR packaging rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does lead-free crystal glassware require FDA approval prior to import?
Glassware does not require pre-market approval, but it is subject to FDA import surveillance and must possess valid test certificates proving compliance with CPG 7117.06/07 heavy metal release limits.
Do solid wooden presentation boxes require ISPM 15 fumigation stamps?
Yes. Solid natural pine wooden boxes shipped internationally require heat treatment and compliant IPPC/ISPM 15 stamping to clear US/EU agricultural inspections. Read our Packaging Specifications Guide for details.
