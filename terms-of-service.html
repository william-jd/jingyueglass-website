B2B Trade Guarantee

Commercial Terms & Quality Warranty

Clear, verifiable fulfillment standards for international glassware procurement, ocean freight protection, and sample fee refund terms.

1. Transit Breakage Guarantee (30-Day Policy)

All export shipments are packaged according to ISTA-1A drop-tested standards with reinforced 5-ply cartons, corner edge protectors, and internal EVA/EPE foam buffering. We provide a comprehensive transit guarantee:

  • Zero Breakage Threshold: For standard orders, transit breakage below 1.0% is covered within standard shipping allowances.
  • 100% Free Replacement / Credit: If breakage exceeds 1.0% upon destination arrival, submit photographic evidence within 30 days of container unloading for immediate free replacement in your next shipment or direct credit refund.

2. Commercial Dishwasher & Food Contact Warranty

🛡️ 1,000+ Wash Cycle Durability Standard

Our machine-blown and hand-blown glassware is guaranteed against clouding, surface pitting, or decal peeling for up to 1,000 commercial wash cycles when washed in standard alkaline commercial dishwashers under recommended hospitality water temperatures (≤ 65°C wash, ≤ 85°C rinse).

All items comply with international safety benchmarks including FDA CPG 545.400 / 545.450 (Report Ref: PTC24100807301C) and California Proposition 65 (Report Ref: SZ2025091217-4E).

3. Rapid Sampling & Fee Refund Protocol

  • Stock Samples: Provided free of charge (client covers express courier freight).
  • Custom Logo / Packaging Proofs: 3–5 working days turnaround. 100% of the custom sampling and tooling fee is fully refunded/deducted from your invoice upon bulk production order confirmation.

4. Production Tolerances for Hand-Crafted Glassware

Due to the artisanal nature of hand-blown lead-free crystal glassware, industry-standard tolerances apply: volumetric capacity ±3%, height/diameter ±2mm, wall thickness ±0.5mm. Machine-blown items feature tightened precision tolerances (±1% capacity).

5. Payment & Delivery Terms

Standard B2B payment terms are 30% deposit upon proforma invoice confirmation and 70% balance payment against Bill of Lading (B/L) copy. Supported Incoterms include EXW, FOB (Shanghai/Shenzhen/Guangzhou), CIF, and DDP (Delivered Duty Paid).
