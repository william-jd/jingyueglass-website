How to Clean & Care for Crystal Wine Decanters

Quick Care Summary Professional Crystal Decanter Cleaning Procedure

Rinse immediately after use with lukewarm water (never exceed 60°C). For tannin deposits, swirl with food-grade stainless steel cleaning beads and mild non-scented detergent for 2 minutes. Air dry completely on a rubber-coated inverted decanter drying stand to prevent water spots and internal clouding.

1. Cleaning Tools & Methods: Stainless Steel Beads vs. Specialty Brushes

Due to the narrow neck and wide aerating bowl of Swan, U-Shape, and Lyre decanters, traditional sponges cannot reach the interior base. Using improper cleaning tools can cause internal scratching or chipping.

Cleaning Method Effectiveness on Tannin Residue Risk of Scratching Recommended Application

💡 Jingyue Lead-Free Crystal Care Advice

Our hand-blown decanters are crafted from high-clarity lead-free crystal glass with a cold-cut, flame-polished rim. To preserve clarity, avoid aggressive alkaline dishwashing detergents and always air-dry upside down on a silicone-tipped decanter drying stand.
