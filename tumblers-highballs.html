FAQ
Common Questions
What is the MOQ for tumblers?
MOQ depends on the selected SKU, carton pack, stock status, logo process and packaging route. The SKU-specific MOQ is listed in the spec sheet above and should be confirmed before quotation.
Can I get tumblers with custom logo printing?
Yes. Custom logo decoration can be reviewed by SKU, artwork size and order quantity. Send your logo file and target position so Jingyue Glass can recommend a suitable process.
Are the tumblers dishwasher safe?
Yes. All our standard tumblers are made from soda-lime glass and are commercial dishwasher safe. For screen-printed logos, we recommend the bottom rack for best print durability.
Do you offer bulk pricing for large orders?
Yes. Pricing should be confirmed by SKU, order quantity, decoration, packaging and shipping basis. The listed FOB references help initial screening but do not replace a formal quotation.
Can you supply to supermarkets in South America?
Export packaging and labeling can be reviewed for the destination market. Please provide barcode, carton mark, label and documentation requirements during quotation.
Which logo methods work for custom logo highball glasses?
Logo method depends on the selected SKU, glass surface, logo size, color count and quantity. Common options can include screen printing, decal, engraving or other decoration routes after artwork review.
What artwork should I send for a highball glass logo quote?
Send a vector file when available, plus the preferred logo width, print position, quantity, packaging plan and destination market. A reference image or mockup also helps confirm placement.
Are all highball glass SKUs compatible with the same logo position?
No. SKU compatibility depends on shape, wall curve, texture and available print area. Jingyue Glass should review the specific SKU before confirming the process and MOQ.
