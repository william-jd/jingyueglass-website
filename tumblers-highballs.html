FAQ
Common Questions
What is the MOQ for tumblers?
Our MOQ is 48 pcs per style for plain tumblers. For custom logo printing, the same MOQ of 48 pcs applies. You can mix different sizes within the same style to reach the MOQ.
Can I get tumblers with custom logo printing?
Yes. We offer screen printing, sandblasting, and decal transfer for custom logos. Send your artwork file and we'll provide a digital mockup within 24 hours at no charge.
Are the tumblers dishwasher safe?
Yes. All our standard tumblers are made from soda-lime glass and are commercial dishwasher safe. For screen-printed logos, we recommend the bottom rack for best print durability.
Do you offer bulk pricing for large orders?
Yes. We offer tiered pricing: the larger your order, the lower the per-piece cost. For orders above 10,000 pcs, please contact us directly for a customized quote.
Can you supply to supermarkets in South America?
Yes. We supply retail chains in South America and are familiar with their packaging and labeling requirements. We can provide barcode-ready packaging and INMETRO-compliant export documentation for Brazil.
