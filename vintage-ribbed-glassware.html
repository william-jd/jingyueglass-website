Buyer Sourcing FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for vintage ribbed glassware?

For existing standard molds and in-stock styles, trial order MOQ starts from 48–96 pcs. For custom brand logo printing or bespoke retail color box packaging, MOQ is typically 300–500 pcs per batch.

Can ribbed glassware withstand high-temperature dishwashing?

Yes. Standard clear fluted glasses without metallic rim detailing are 100% commercial dishwasher safe. For glasses with gold rim or electroplated finishes, gentle hand washing is recommended to preserve luster.

How does Jingyue Glass prevent breakage during sea freight?

Every shipment is packaged with 5-ply corrugated export cartons, heavy-duty egg-crate dividers, and optional high-density foam padding that passes standard ISTA-1A drop tests.
