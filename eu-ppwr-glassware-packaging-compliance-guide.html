Understanding the EU Packaging & Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR)

The European Union's Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) introduces mandatory recyclability criteria, minimum recycled content thresholds, and strict restrictions on single-use plastic packaging and excessive empty space ratios. For B2B glassware importers in Germany, France, Italy, and across the EU, non-compliant packaging risks customs holds, EPR eco-modulation penalties, and retailer rejection.
