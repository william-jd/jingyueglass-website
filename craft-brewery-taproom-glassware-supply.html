Taproom Procurement FAQs

What is the fastest way to get physical branded beer glass samples?
For urgent tasting room openings or retail approvals, choose Laser Engraving or UV Digital Printing. We complete physical proofs in 1–3 working days and dispatch via DHL/FedEx Air Express (3–5 days transit to North America & Europe).
How durable is the 580°C High-Temp Decals for commercial bar dishwashers?
Our ceramic enamels undergo high-temperature annealing at 580°C, melting the pigment directly into the silica matrix. They withstand over 1,000 commercial wash cycles and caustic chemical sanitizers without peeling, clouding, or scratching.
