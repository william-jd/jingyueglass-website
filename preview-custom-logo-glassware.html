How to Launch a Custom Logo Glassware Project

From vector artwork to master carton container loading in 4 clear, quality-assured steps.

01. Select Glass SKU

Pick from our in-stock whiskey glasses, wine stems, carafes, or double-wall mugs, or submit custom volume & height requirements.

02. Vector Artwork & Mockup

Send your logo in .AI / .PDF / .SVG format. Our engineering team renders a scaled 2D/3D digital placement proof within 24 hours.

03. Physical Pre-Production Sample

We execute physical laser proofing (1-3 days) or high-temp decal proofing (7-10 days) and send 4K inspection videos and physical samples.

04. Mass Production & AQL 2.5 QC

Upon sample sign-off, mass decoration proceeds with strict AQL 2.5 inspection, carton drop-testing, and export palletization.

Frequently Asked Questions

Custom Logo Glassware FAQs

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom logo glassware?

For standard in-stock crystal and borosilicate glassware, fiber laser logo engraving starts at just 48 pcs with a 1-3 working day sample turnaround. UV full-color printing requires an MOQ of 100 pcs, 580°C dishwasher-safe high-temp decals require 500 pcs, and full custom OEM private molds start from 3,000 pcs with 100% mold fee refund on qualifying volume.

Are Jingyue Glass decorated logos dishwasher-safe and food contact compliant?

Yes. Our fiber laser engraving is a permanent physical etching that never fades or peels under any washing conditions. Our 580°C High-Temp Decals fuse inorganic pigments into the glass matrix, passing 1,000+ commercial dishwashing cycles and meeting FDA CPG 7117.06/07, California Proposition 65, and EU LFGB food-contact safety standards.

What file formats are required to start a custom logo project?

Please provide vector artwork in Adobe Illustrator (.AI), PDF, EPS, or SVG format with fonts outlined. If specific color matching is required for decals or screen printing, please specify Pantone (PMS) color codes. Our engineering team provides a 2D/3D digital proof within 24 hours free of charge.

Can you produce custom retail and corporate gift packaging alongside logo glassware?

Yes. We offer complete turnkey packaging solutions including single matte black kraft boxes, satin-lined rigid gift boxes with high-density EVA foam inserts, cylindrical craft tubes, and ISTA-1A drop-tested Amazon FBA master shipper cartons.
