Commercial Specifications & Quality Standards

Model XZ5202 is manufactured using high-precision automated machine-blowing lines with seamless pulled stem technology. By integrating the bowl and stem from a single gather of molten crystal, this model eliminates the weak joint line inherent in multi-piece glued stems, dramatically reducing commercial dishwashing breakage rates.
