What is the MOQ for wine decanters?
Our MOQ is 30 pcs for standard in-stock designs. Custom mold decanters require a minimum of 500 pcs. You can mix different decanter styles in one shipment.
Can I get my logo engraved on the decanter?
Yes. We offer laser engraving, sandblasting, screen printing, and color painting. Send your logo file (AI, PDF, or PNG) and we'll provide a mockup within 1 hour at no charge.
Do you supply complete decanter gift sets?
Absolutely. We offer decanter + 2 glasses and decanter + 4 glasses sets, packed in 4-color retail gift boxes. This is one of our strongest product lines for wholesale buyers.
Are your decanters lead-free?
Yes. All our decanters are manufactured from lead-free glass and tested to FDA and LFGB standards. Full test reports are available upon request.
How do you ship decanters safely?
Each decanter is individually wrapped in foam and packed in reinforced cartons. Our breakage rate for international sea freight is below 0.5%. All shipments are insured.
Do you ship to South America?
Yes — we regularly ship to Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Argentina, Peru and across Southeast Asia and Europe. We provide all export documents for smooth customs clearance.

Frequently Asked Procurement Questions

Key commercial terms, quality standards, and OEM customization guidelines for international buyers.

What glass material is used for your wine decanters and aerators?

Our sculptural and U-shaped wine decanters are hand-blown from ultra-clear lead-free acoustic crystal glass or thermal shock-resistant high-borosilicate glass (COE 3.3), ensuring brilliant optical clarity and zero heavy metal leaching.

Can decanters be customized with private label logos and wooden bases?

Yes, we offer precision laser etching, sandblasting, and bespoke accessories including custom natural oak wood ball stoppers, stainless steel drying stands, and cleaning bead sets.

What is the packaging standard for delicate crystal decanters?

Decanters are secured inside precision-cut odorless EPE pearl cotton cradles encased in 1200g rigid gift boxes or solid pine slide-lid boxes, rigorously tested to pass 1.2M courier drop tests with zero breakage.

What is the production lead time for bulk decanter orders?

Pre-production physical samples take 3–5 working days. Mass production for 1,000–5,000 units is typically completed within 20–30 days with express or ocean shipping support.
