FAQ
Common Questions
What is the MOQ for wine decanters?
Our MOQ is 30 pcs for standard in-stock designs. Custom mold decanters require a minimum of 500 pcs. You can mix different decanter styles in one shipment.
Can I get my logo engraved on the decanter?
Yes. We offer laser engraving, sandblasting, screen printing, and color painting. Send your logo file (AI, PDF, or PNG) and we'll provide a mockup within 1 hour at no charge.
Do you supply complete decanter gift sets?
Absolutely. We offer decanter + 2 glasses and decanter + 4 glasses sets, packed in 4-color retail gift boxes. This is one of our strongest product lines for wholesale buyers.
Are your decanters lead-free?
Yes. All our decanters are manufactured from lead-free glass and tested to FDA and LFGB standards. Full test reports are available upon request.
How do you ship decanters safely?
Each decanter is individually wrapped in foam and packed in reinforced cartons. Our breakage rate for international sea freight is below 0.5%. All shipments are insured.
Do you ship to South America?
Yes — we regularly ship to Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Argentina, Peru and across Southeast Asia and Europe. We provide all export documents for smooth customs clearance.
