FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ and sampling lead time for custom non-stock gift sets vs in-stock sets? +
For custom non-stock gift sets (bespoke box dimensions, specialty wood stains, custom EVA inserts), the MOQ starts from 100 sets with a sampling lead time of 5–20 working days. For in-stock gift sets requiring custom laser logo branding only, sample dispatch is completed within 1 business day upon stock verification and vector artwork confirmation.
How is sample pricing structured compared to bulk production prices and shipping times? +
Physical sample kits range from $10 to $200 per set depending on set complexity, materials (solid wood chests vs magnetic boxes), and accessories. Sample costs and courier air transit (3–7 days) are quoted separately from bulk FOB container pricing and ocean transit. Sample fees are 100% credited against your subsequent bulk order invoice.
Can you engrave individual employee or client names on each glass/box? +
Yes. We offer variable laser engraving for personalized VIP and executive gifts, allowing custom recipient names across wood boxes and crystal glasses.
Can we include custom marketing inserts, cards, or user guides inside the gift boxes? +
Absolutely. We provide full fulfillment services including printing and inserting custom greeting cards, cocktail recipe booklets, and branded sleeve packaging.
