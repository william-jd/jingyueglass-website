Custom Glassware Packaging & ISTA-1A Drop-Test Protocol for Fragile Export

By Jingyue Glass Quality Assurance Team • Published October 2026 • ISTA-1A / ISO 9001 / Amazon SIOC Compliant

Breakage during multi-modal ocean and cross-border e-commerce transport is the primary risk factor for commercial glassware importers. This technical SOP outlines Jingyue Glass's Odorless High-Density EPE Pearl Cotton cushioning standards, 5-Ply Double-Wall Corrugated (BC-Flute) Master Cartons, and the standardized ISTA-1A 10-Drop Testing Sequence that keeps export breakage rates below 0.3%.

1. The Four-Tier Engineered Packaging Architecture

Standard export packaging is engineered in four interlocking protective zones to isolate fragile crystal and borosilicate glassware from compression shock and vibration fatigue:

Protection Layer Material & Specification Engineering Function Compliance Standard
Layer 1: Primary Contact Individual Polybag / Tissue Wrap Dust prevention & zero surface scratching during transit. Food-grade LDPE (Recyclable)
Layer 2: Shock Isolation Odorless CNC-Molded EPE Pearl Cotton (28kg/m³) 360° customized contour cavities with ≥15mm side clearance; zero chemical off-gassing. REACH / RoHS compliant
Layer 3: Retail/Gift Box 1200g Rigid Greyboard + 157g Art Paper Crush resistance (≥1200 kPa) and luxury tactile branding. FSC Certified Board
Layer 4: Export Master Carton 5-Ply Double-Wall Corrugated (BC-Flute, 250g/140g/120g/140g/250g) Stacking burst strength ≥14 kg/cm² (Edge Crush Test ≥ 8.5 kN/m). ISTA-1A / Amazon SIOC Type A

2. Why Odorless EPE Pearl Cotton is Mandatory (vs. Traditional EVA/Sponge)

🌿 Zero Chemical Odor Guarantee for Food-Contact Drinkware
Traditional EVA foam and open-cell polyurethane sponges release volatile organic compounds (VOCs) that trap chemical odors inside enclosed cups. Jingyue Glass standardizes on high-purity, thermally bonded EPE pearl cotton that is 100% odorless, non-toxic, and certified for food-contact luxury gift presentation.

3. The ISTA-1A 10-Drop Impact Verification Sequence

Every newly developed SKU or bespoke gift box configuration undergoes a standardized 10-drop impact test from a calibrated height of 76 cm (30 inches) onto flat concrete:

  1. Drop 1: Most vulnerable corner (Box Corner 5-1-2).
  2. Drops 2–4: Three adjacent shortest edges radiating from the dropped corner.
  3. Drops 5–10: All six flat faces (Bottom, Top, Front, Back, Right, Left).

Pass Criteria: Zero micro-cracks in glassware, zero stem detachment, and zero box structural rupture.

4. 48-Hour Breakage Compensation Policy

Jingyue Glass provides guaranteed breakage protection: in the rare event of transit damage exceeding the 0.5% standard threshold, buyers simply submit unboxing photos or 4K videos within 48 hours of container receipt for 100% credit or immediate free replacement dispatch.
