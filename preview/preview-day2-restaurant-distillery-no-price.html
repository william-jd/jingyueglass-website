Technical project workflow

Confirm engineering details before making claims

Distillery applications can require closer fit, decoration and packaging review than standard service glassware.

Drawings & dimensions

Provide closure drawings, intended fill volume, line constraints and the measurement points that matter. Final tolerances are to be confirmed.

Tooling & prototypes

Share shape, embossing and target-use requirements. CAD, prototype and mold schedules are to be confirmed.

Decoration validation

Identify artwork, process, contact conditions and cleaning method. Resistance and wash-cycle scope are to be confirmed.

Packaging review

Define bottle, glass or set configuration, insert, outer carton, marks and transport route before a packaging plan is proposed.

Fields deliberately not stated as fixed facts

  • Neck or closure tolerances
  • CAD, prototype and private-mold timing
  • Commercial minimum quantities
  • Wash-cycle or alcohol-resistance counts
  • Certification coverage by SKU and destination
  • Leakproof, airtight or zero-evaporation guarantees
  • Sample dispatch and quotation response times
All to be confirmed
Buyer questions

What happens before quotation and sampling?

Can several restaurant and bar categories be included in one RFQ?

Yes. List each requested SKU or glass type, capacity and quantity separately, then add logo, packaging, destination and delivery requirements. Feasibility and commercial terms are reviewed item by item.

Can a distillery request a custom logo or embossed concept?

Submit artwork, placement, desired process, quantity and target use. Jingyue can review decoration or tooling routes, but feasibility, commercial minimums, samples and timing remain to be confirmed.

Can you confirm a decanter closure tolerance or tooling schedule now?

Not from the current evidence set. Provide drawings and project requirements so the dimensional scope, sample plan and schedule can be confirmed for the selected configuration.

Request an itemized quotation or sample plan

Send quantity by SKU, capacity, logo artwork, packaging configuration, destination and requested delivery window. The response can then separate product, decoration, packaging, sampling and delivery requirements.
