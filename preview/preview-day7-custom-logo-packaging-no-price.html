SOURCING & PACKAGING FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions on Custom Packaging & Branding

Why does Jingyue Glass use odorless EPE pearl cotton instead of EVA foam?

Traditional EVA foam releases strong volatile chemical odors (off-gassing) that ruin the aroma of fine whiskey and wine upon opening. Our CNC-molded high-density EPE pearl cotton (38°–45° shore hardness) is 100% odorless, non-toxic, and offers superior shock absorption during international transit.

How do you verify packaging protection before bulk container dispatch?

Every packaging design undergoes rigorous ISTA-1A drop testing (1 corner, 3 edges, and 6 faces dropped from 1.2 meters onto solid concrete). We only approve box structures that achieve a 100% zero-breakage result.

Can I order custom wooden gift boxes with small batch test quantities?

Yes. For standard models like Model XZM015 (pine wooden box whiskey set) and Model XZM108-A (paulownia wooden box wine set), our private label MOQ starts from only 100 sets, allowing brand owners to test new products with minimal capital commitment.

What vector files are required for logo proofs and how fast are samples dispatched?

We accept AI, PDF, EPS, and SVG vector artwork. Digital 3D rendering mockups are generated within 1 hour, and physical pre-production samples with custom laser logos or decals are dispatched in 3–5 working days.
