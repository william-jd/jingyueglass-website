Panel 03 · Custom packaging

Build the box around the real decorated product.

Packaging is presented as a specification conversation: box structure, insert material, artwork, labels, carton protection, quantity and destination are reviewed together. Wooden boxes and gift boxes use odorless EPE pearl cotton for the inner protection requirement in this preview.

Export carton and mailer route

For courier handling and export movement. Confirm master carton, dividers, marks, label content and destination expectations.

Packaging RFQ checklist

Five inputs reduce avoidable rework.

Product and set configuration
Glass SKU, quantity per set, accessories, dimensions and weight.
Box structure and artwork
Reference photo or dieline, material, finish, logo area and language.
Insert specification
Fit, cavities, divider needs and odorless EPE pearl cotton requirement.
Carton and labels
Units per carton, carton marks, barcode or importer label content.
Destination and channel
Retail, gifting, ecommerce or hospitality; country, city and shipping context.
Compliance boundary
Keep odorless EPE pearl cotton as inner protection; do not describe it as PPWR plastic film.
Pack-out review

Approve the decorated item inside its final protection.

A packaging sample should be checked with the logo, coating or decoration applied. This confirms clearance, fit, presentation and handling assumptions before a scale order.

Buyer FAQ

Common questions, without public price claims.

What is needed for an itemized custom quote?

Send the artwork, selected glassware or set configuration, decoration route, quantity, packaging structure, insert requirement and destination. Jingyue can then review the complete project route.

Can packaging be reviewed with decorated glassware?

Yes. Packaging should be checked with the decorated product, final dimensions, insert fit, carton requirements and destination handling expectations.

Can I request catalogs and specifications before an RFQ?

Yes. Request the relevant catalog and specifications package and identify the product family, compliance documents or packaging information needed for your sourcing review.
