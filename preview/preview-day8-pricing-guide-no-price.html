Branding Methods

2. Custom Decoration & Branding Technical Overview

We evaluate your vector artwork, target durability, color requirements, and volume to recommend the optimal decoration process.

🔥 Precision Fiber Laser

Permanently micro-etched mark into the glass surface. 100% scratch-proof and commercial dishwasher safe.

  • Suitability: Monochrome logos, serial numbers, fine linework
  • Durability: Permanent, lifetime wear resistance
  • Sampling: Digital proofing & rapid physical verification

🎨 UV Digital Color Print

High-resolution CMYK gradients and vibrant multi-color brand graphics with precise alignment.

  • Suitability: Full-color brand artwork & gradient graphics
  • Durability: High daily-use adhesion
  • Sampling: Rapid digital setup & color match proofing

🏆 580°C High-Temp Decals

Silkscreened inorganic ceramic enamels fired at 580°C to fuse permanently into the glass structure.

  • Suitability: Commercial hospitality & high-volume retail
  • Durability: Withstands 1,000+ commercial wash cycles
  • Sampling: Custom screen preparation & kiln proofing

🛠️ OEM Private Mold Tooling

Custom glass silhouette, proprietary embossed bottom branding, or unique geometric wall textures.

  • Suitability: Exclusive brand identity & proprietary product lines
  • Durability: Structural glass feature
  • Sampling: 3D CAD modeling & physical sample mold trial

Frequently Asked Questions

4. B2B Commercial & Logistics Inquiries

What payment and commercial trade terms are supported?

Standard export trade terms include FOB Wuhan/Shanghai, CIF destination seaport/airport, and DDP door-to-door delivery. Standard commercial terms follow 30% T/T deposit upon order confirmation and 70% balance against shipping documents, with Alibaba Trade Assurance and L/C at sight supported for qualifying volume contracts.

Do you provide DDP shipping to commercial warehouses or Amazon FBA?

Yes. We coordinate comprehensive DDP sea, air, and rail freight solutions including customs entry, port handling, and domestic final-mile delivery to designated 3PL facilities, corporate offices, or Amazon fulfillment centers with all import tariffs coordinated.

How is zero breakage guaranteed during ocean container transport?

All packaging configurations are engineered against ISTA-1A drop test standards, utilizing 5-ply corrugated export master cartons, rigid internal partition grids, and custom odorless EPE pearl cotton inserts for premium sets. All shipments adhere to rigorous AQL 2.5 outgoing quality standards.
