3. Synchronized section blueprint

Preferred industry-page structure

  1. Hero: “Commercial Hospitality Glassware Supply for Hotels & Restaurants” with a no-price procurement CTA.
  2. Quick answer: select durable soda-lime or lead-free crystal by traffic, service style, optical requirement and washing environment.
  3. Application matrix: hotels, restaurants, bars, banquet rental and catering; show use cases, not price.
  4. Specification/RFQ block: SKU, capacity, rim, base, decoration, carton pack, destination and inspection requirements.
  5. Packaging block: evidence-backed EPE wording only for confirmed packaging configurations; otherwise say “packaging route to be confirmed.”
  6. FAQ: retain useful buyer questions but remove absolute guarantees and unsupported certification language.
  7. Final CTA: send an itemized RFQ or request a catalog/specification pack.
