Cost Driver Framework

Five dimensions that shape a B2B glassware quotation

Each project is reviewed against its technical specification and logistics route. The model below replaces generic public price anchors with an itemized, scope-led RFQ conversation.

01 · Material

Base Material & Structure

Material chemistry and forming route influence clarity, weight, thermal behavior and manufacturing complexity.

  • Lead-Free Crystal: clarity, premium weight and stem geometry
  • Borosilicate: thermal-shock performance and double-wall construction
  • Soda-Lime Glass: scalable everyday drinkware and commercial use
02 · Volume

Purchase Volume & Cartonization

Order scale and shipment architecture affect production planning, carton utilization and handling efficiency.

  • Master Carton: inner count, dividers and pallet pattern
  • LCL: consolidation, cubic volume and destination handling
  • FCL 40HQ: container utilization and loading plan
03 · Decoration

Customization & Decoration

Artwork coverage, color count, firing and manual finishing define the process route.

  • Laser engraving and Screen Print
  • 580°C high-temperature Decal
  • Gold Rim and hand-painted spray finishes
04 · Packaging

Packaging Specification

Protection, presentation and fulfillment requirements are quoted as a defined pack-out system.

  • Export-standard 5-layer outer carton
  • Odorless EPE pearl-cotton insert gift box
  • Drop-resistant single-piece e-commerce mailer
05 · Destination

Trade Terms & Destination

Incoterm, route and destination determine handover scope, documentation and delivery coordination.

  • FOB Wuhan / Shanghai
  • CIF nominated port or airport
  • DDP by air or sea to the agreed address

Packaging & Decoration Detail

Define the specification before comparing offers

Decoration route selection

Match the finish to the artwork, wear environment and brand hierarchy.

  • Laser: precise monochrome marks and traceable identifiers.
  • Screen Print: controlled spot colors and repeatable logo coverage.
  • 580°C Decal: ceramic decoration for a fused, high-durability finish.
  • Gold Rim: metallic accent requiring rim geometry and handling controls.
  • Hand-painted spray: artisanal gradients or coverage requiring sample sign-off.

Protection route selection

State whether the shipment is for wholesale replenishment, retail shelf presentation or parcel fulfillment.

  • Export pack: 5-layer outer carton with internal partitions.
  • Gift presentation: odorless EPE pearl-cotton insert with fitted cavities.
  • E-commerce: drop-resistant single-piece mailer for parcel handling.
  • Confirm carton markings, barcode placement, pallet rules and drop-test expectations.
RFQ Preparation Checklist

Submit a complete brief for a faster itemized review

Attach or confirm the fields below. Unknown fields can be marked “supplier recommendation requested” so assumptions remain visible.

  • Product identitySKU, reference image or drawing, capacity, dimensions and target use.
  • Material and structureLead-Free Crystal, Borosilicate or Soda-Lime; rim, base, wall and thermal requirements.
  • Order planInitial quantity, SKU mix, replenishment cadence and preferred Master Carton count.
  • Artwork packageAI, PDF, EPS or SVG files, placement, dimensions, colors and finish reference.
  • Packaging brief5-layer export carton, odorless EPE gift box or e-commerce single-piece mailer.
  • Quality and testingAppearance standard, AQL reference, dishwasher use, drop test and sample acceptance criteria.
  • Trade and destinationFOB Wuhan/Shanghai, CIF or DDP air/sea; destination port, warehouse and importer details.
  • Timing and documentsSample review date, production window, shipping window, labeling and compliance files.
Sample & Approval SOP

From technical brief to production release

Keep one approved reference across the buyer, decoration team, packaging team and final inspection.

Brief intake

Confirm the five dimensions, collect artwork and flag missing decisions or supplier recommendations.

Sample route

Choose blank, decorated, packaging or combined sampling based on the risk that must be evaluated.

Proof and review

Check color, registration, rim finish, dimensions, weight, insert fit, carton marks and handling notes.

Approval and release

Record written approval, lock the reference sample and release production only against the confirmed brief.

Transparent sample policy

Sample scope, courier responsibility, decoration setup, packaging proofing and any tooling or engineering treatment are confirmed per project brief. No fixed sample or tooling charge is published on this preview; the formal response will itemize applicable scope for buyer approval.

Buyer FAQ

Commercial and logistics questions

What should be included in a glassware RFQ?

Include material and structure, target quantity and carton plan, decoration artwork and process, packaging protection, destination and requested Incoterm, quality standard, schedule and sample approval requirements.

Which trade terms can be reviewed?

FOB Wuhan or Shanghai, CIF to the nominated destination, and DDP by air or sea can be reviewed against the product, packaging, destination and import requirements.

How does sample approval work?

The buyer confirms the technical brief, reviews the recommended blank or decorated sample route, approves artwork and packaging proofs, records comments and signs the final reference sample before production release.
